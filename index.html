Love Story

1

🥙 Unexpected Meeting

The tough year of living abroad for Shabrina was the same year that Sakib came to Japan for work. After talking in the phone, they decided to meet because Sakib made Shabrina laugh😀 They went to eat kebab at the park next to Shabrina's best friend's apartment, Eka. "I can see you guys from my room window," Eka wrote in the chat. Shabrina then called her and said, "Come here!"

2

💞 Cultural Difference and Communication

Sakib and Shabrina were getting along quickly. However, it wasn't always all rainbows because they often had misunderstandings due to cultural differences..😞 Shabrina is from Indonesia, while Sakib is from Bangladesh and Afghanistan. Slowly but surely, they learned that difference is not a reason for separation, but a chance to deepen understanding and respect for each other..♥️

3

🇮🇩 Sakib's Visit to Indonesia

After 1.5 year of being in relationship, to get to know Shabrina's family and culture, Sakib went to Indonesia. They also traveled to Bandung🚄 with Shabrina's father, brother, and cousin, and attended the wedding of Shabrina's brother. Sakib loves Indonesian food so much, especially rendang, bebek, and ayam bakar! But please don't give him spicy sambal..🥵

4

🇧🇩 Shabrina's Visit to Bangladesh

During summer holiday, it was Shabrina's turn to fly to Bangladesh, accompanied by her brother. Sakib's sisters welcomed her with flower bouquets 💐 Shabrina also showed off her Bengali skill to Sakib's mom, but she still needs to learn more🤣 In Dhaka, she was so amused with the beautiful Mughal architecture.. by the way, Bengali girls and women proudly wear their traditional attire in daily life, so she also bought pink saree for herself 🥰

5

💍 Planning for Future

With the blessing of their family, Sakib and Shabrina are planning for their new chapter of life. They are very excited for what's coming ahead🥰 After their nikah ceremony in Tokyo, insyaallah they will hold a wedding reception in Indonesia and Bangladesh to celebrate with their beloved family and friends.
